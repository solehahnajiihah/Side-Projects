## Introduction 
Airbnb has quickly become one of the most successful business, introduced by Brian Chesky in 2008. It provides an online marketplace that offers a short and long term homestays in multiple countries and regions.
According to Renascence, Airbnb uses customer-oriented business strategy, offering unique and local experiences and personalized host interactions. These approaches set them apart from their competitors by focusing on authentic and local experiences which allow guests to immerse in the community.

## Objective 
Hosts do not know where, when and what type of listings generate the highest returns in NYC.

The purpose of this project are as follows:
1. Identify the most popular neighbourhood for hosting.
2. Analyze reviews per month across neighbourhoods and room types to understand demand patterns.
3. Analyze monthly and yearly trends to identify seasonal demand shifts.

## Data
The data is obtained from Dgomonov through [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/dgomonov/new-york-city-airbnb-open-data) website.

## Conclusion
- Manhattan is the most profitable Airbnb market in New York City, driven by consistently demand and significantly higher average prices compared to other neighbourhoods. 
- Customer booking behavior shows a clear preference for entire home/apartment and private room listings, while shared rooms attract lower demand due to limited privacy. To maximize occupancy and revenue, hosts should prioritize offering these room types, particularly to accommodate families and groups of travelers.

- Airbnb demand in New York City shows strong seasonality, with bookings peaking during the summer months of June and July, likely driven by school and summer holidays. Hosts can capitalize on this trend by adjusting pricing strategies and increasing listing availability during peak periods to optimize revenue.
