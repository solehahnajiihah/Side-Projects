## Introduction 
Airbnb has quickly become one of the most successful business, introduced by Brian Chesky in 2008. It provides an online marketplace that offers a short and long term homestays in multiple countries and regions.
According to Renascence, Airbnb uses customer-oriented business strategy, offering unique and local experiences and personalized host interactions. These approaches set them apart from their competitors by focusing on authentic and local experiences which allow guests to immerse in the community.

## Objective 
The purpose of this project are to make analysis of the data and investigate areas with the highest traffic hosts.
